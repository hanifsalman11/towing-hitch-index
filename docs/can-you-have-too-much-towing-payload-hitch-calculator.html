Frequently Asked Questions (Can You Have Too Much)

What is the realistic camper weight a Can You Have Too Much can safely tow?

While the maximum brochure tow rating is 8,500 lbs, experienced towing engineers follow the 80% safety rule (6,800 lbs loaded GVWR). At 6,800 lbs, the 13% tongue weight is 884 lbs, keeping you safely inside the 1,750 lb rear axle/payload limit.

Do I need a Weight Distribution Hitch (WDH) on the Can You Have Too Much?

Yes. Most Class IV (2-Inch) factory receivers are rated for only 500 lbs of dead-weight tongue load (5,000 lb trailer) without weight distribution. Any trailer exceeding 5,000 lbs requires a 1,000 lb Spring Bar Weight Distribution Hitch with 4-Point Sway Control.

How does passenger and cargo weight affect towing capacity on the Can You Have Too Much?

Every pound of tongue weight (884 lbs) and hitch hardware (~90 lbs) is subtracted directly from your 1,750 lb truck payload rating. If you carry 776+ lbs of passengers, firewood, and coolers in the truck, you must reduce trailer tongue weight accordingly.
